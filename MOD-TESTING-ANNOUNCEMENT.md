Introducing **GAW: RE-SEARCH** — a free tool for digging the GAW archive.

**What it is:** a Chrome extension that searches the *entire* greatawakening.win
archive — 130,000+ posts and comments and growing — instantly, with no login and
no tracking. Built for research: find old posts and comments by keyword, author,
date, score, flair, whatever you're chasing.

**How to install it (2 minutes):**
1. Download the ZIP: `gaw-research-search-v2.4.0.zip` (link below).
2. Unzip it anywhere on your computer.
3. Open Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions`
4. Turn on **Developer mode** (top-right toggle).
5. Click **Load unpacked**, and select the unzipped folder.
6. A new ⚖ icon appears in your toolbar — that's it, you're in.

**The basics:**
- Type anything and hit Search. That's the whole learning curve.
- Open **Filters** to narrow by date (quick presets: 24h / 7 days / 30 days /
  custom), author, score, flair, comment count, and sort order.
- Star ★ a search to save it; recent searches show as one-tap chips.
- **Copy results** grabs your current results as a tidy list you can paste
  anywhere.
- Can't find a post? **⊕ Add a Post** — paste its link and it's indexed on the
  spot.

**Advanced Mode (flip the switch up top):** power search for serious digging —
an **exact phrase** field, **any of these words** (OR), and **exclude these
words** (NOT), with a live preview of the query it builds. If you've ever used
Google's advanced search, you already know how it works.

**Privacy:** No accounts, no login, no tracking, no analytics. Your saved and
recent searches live on your own computer only. The only thing that leaves
your machine is your search query, sent to our proxy to fetch results. Full
